When selecting a battery for a drone, consider the following key factors:  

### **1. Types of Drone Batteries**  
- **Lithium Polymer (LiPo):** Most common due to high energy density, lightweight, and high discharge rates.  
- **Lithium-Ion (Li-Ion):** Higher energy density than LiPo but lower discharge rates, suitable for long-endurance flights.  
- **Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH):** Less common, heavier, and lower energy density, but safer.  
- **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4):** Safer and more durable than LiPo, but heavier and lower energy density.  

### **2. Key Specifications**  
- **Voltage (V):** Determined by the number of cells in series (S). Each LiPo cell has 3.7V nominal voltage. Common configurations:
  - 1S (3.7V)  
  - 2S (7.4V)  
  - 3S (11.1V)  
  - 4S (14.8V) (Standard for racing drones)  
  - 6S (22.2V) (For high-performance drones)  
- **Capacity (mAh or Ah):** Determines flight duration. Higher capacity = longer flight time but more weight.  
- **C-Rating (Discharge Rate):** Indicates how fast a battery can release energy. Higher C-rating is needed for high-power drones.  
- **Energy Density:** LiPo batteries have a typical range of **150-250 Wh/kg**.  

### **3. Choosing the Right Battery for Your Drone**  
- **Racing Drones:** Prefer **4S or 6S LiPo with high C-rating (≥50C)**.  
- **Photography Drones:** Need **higher capacity (5000mAh+) for longer flights**.  
- **Industrial & Delivery Drones:** Use **high-capacity Li-Ion for endurance**.  
- **FPV Drones:** Require **lightweight, high-discharge LiPo** for agility.  

### **4. Battery Safety & Maintenance**  
- Always use a **balanced charger** to avoid overcharging.  
- Store at **50% charge** for longevity.  
- Avoid **puncturing or overheating** LiPo batteries.  
- Use **fireproof bags** for storage and charging.
