**Flight Time Calculation**
   - For each stage:
     \[
     \text{Time}_{\text{stage}} = \frac{(1 - M) \times C_{\text{nominal}}}{I_{\text{stage}}}
     \]
     Where:
     - \( M \): Safety margin (typically 10–20%)
     - \( C_{\text{nominal}} \): Battery capacity in Ah
     - \( I_{\text{stage}} \): Current draw during that stage
