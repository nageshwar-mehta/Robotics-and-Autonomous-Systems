### 🔋 **Stage-wise Flight Time Formula with Safety Margin**

#### ✅ **Formula**:
\[
\boxed{
\text{Time}_{\text{stage}} = \frac{(1 - M) \times C_{\text{nominal}}}{I_{\text{stage}}}
}
\]

#### 📌 **Definitions**:
| Symbol               | Description                                      |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| \( C_{\text{nominal}} \) | Nominal battery capacity in Ah               |
| \( M \)              | Safety margin (decimal form, e.g., 0.1 for 10%) |
| \( I_{\text{stage}} \) | Current draw during flight stage in Amperes    |
| \( \text{Time}_{\text{stage}} \) | Estimated duration of that stage in hours     |

---

### 🧮 **Example Calculation (10% Margin)**

- Given:  
  \( C_{\text{nominal}} = 2.2 \, \text{Ah} \)  
  \( M = 0.1 \quad (\text{10% safety margin}) \)  
  \( I_{\text{hover}} = 15 \, \text{A} \)

- Step 1:  
  \[
  C_{\text{usable}} = (1 - 0.1) \times 2.2 = 1.98 \, \text{Ah}
  \]

- Step 2:  
  \[
  \text{Time}_{\text{hover}} = \frac{1.98}{15} = 0.132 \, \text{hr} = \boxed{7.9 \, \text{minutes}}
  \]
